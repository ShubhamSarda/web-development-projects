About Urban Nest

Our Story

Beautiful interiors begin with understanding people

Urban Nest was founded on a simple idea: the best interiors are the ones that feel deeply personal. We design spaces that support the way you live, work, gather, and unwind—without sacrificing beauty or practicality.

From city apartments and family homes to boutique hospitality spaces, our studio blends thoughtful planning, layered textures, and timeless materials to create rooms that feel elevated, warm, and effortlessly lived in.
